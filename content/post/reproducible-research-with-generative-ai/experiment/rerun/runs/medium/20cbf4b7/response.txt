So responses after unrelated primes were about 18.5 ms slower than after related primes, with a 95% Wald CI of -0.74 to 37.78 ms. The interval includes zero, so the priming effect is not reliably different from zero at the .05 level.

lme4 reported the fit as singular ("boundary (singular) fit"). The optimiser still converged (code 0). I kept the specified model unchanged.

What the script does:
- Reads all 1,440 rows of data.csv, with no exclusions and rt left as raw milliseconds.
- Checks that prime only takes the values related or unrelated.
- Creates prime_c, coded -0.5 for related and +0.5 for unrelated.
- Fits `lmer(rt ~ prime_c + (1 + prime_c | participant) + (1 + prime_c | item))` with REML and the default optimiser, and prints the model summary.
- Takes the fixed effect of prime_c as the estimate and the interval from `confint(fit, parm = "prime_c", method = "Wald")`, then prints the RESULT line last.
